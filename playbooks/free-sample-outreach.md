# Playbook: Free-Sample Outreach Engine

_The core lead engine. It finds leads AND builds portfolio with every run — even the ones who never reply._

**Owner review required:** during setup, Claude drafts everything and sends nothing until approved.

## The logic

Cold outreach with a free finished clip attached converts far better than "hey I'm a video editor." The sample removes all risk for the creator and instantly proves skill. And because we make the clip regardless of whether they reply, **every send grows our portfolio**. Leads and proof from the same action.

## Steps

### 1. Pick a target
Pull the next creator that fits the [ICP](../business/ideal-customer.md) from the pipeline, or find a new one (prospecting tools / YouTube / podcast charts). Confirm they have long-form content and something to sell.

### 2. Find their content + contact
- Grab a recent long-form video/episode with good clip potential (a strong story, hot take, or emotional moment).
- Find a real contact path: business email, booking/contact page, or DM handle.

### 3. Make the clip (Descript)
- Cut one 30–60s vertical short around the single best moment.
- Add captions and a strong hook in the first 2 seconds.
- Keep it genuinely good — this clip represents us.

### 4. Log it in the portfolio *immediately*
Add an entry to [`portfolio/`](../portfolio/) with the source, the clip, and a note. **Do this whether or not they ever reply** — this is how the track record rebuilds.

### 5. Draft the outreach
Use a template from [`outreach/cold-email-templates.md`](../outreach/cold-email-templates.md). Personalize: reference the specific episode and moment. Lead with the free clip, low-pressure ask.

### 6. Owner reviews → send
Owner approves the draft and the clip. Then send (email/DM). Record the send in the pipeline.

### 7. Follow up
- No reply in ~3–4 days → send follow-up #1 (from templates).
- Still nothing in ~5–7 more days → follow-up #2, then mark dormant.
- Reply → move to conversation; goal is a call or a "yes, send more" → [Rung 1 retainer](../business/offers-and-pricing.md).

### 8. Update the pipeline
Every touch updates [`crm/pipeline.md`](../crm/pipeline.md) — stage, last action, next action, date.

## Definition of a good run

- 1 real clip made and **logged in portfolio**.
- 1 personalized draft ready for review.
- Pipeline updated with the next action.

## Cadence (once trusted)

Batch it: find 5 targets → make 5 clips → 5 drafts → review → send → follow-ups tracked automatically. This is a strong candidate to later run as a scheduled routine (e.g. a weekly batch), once the owner trusts the output.
