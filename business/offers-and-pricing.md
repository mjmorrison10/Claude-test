# Offers & Pricing (the upsell ladder)

_Draft pricing — adjust to what the market and your confidence support. Start lower to land proof, raise as the portfolio grows._

## Rung 0 — Free Sample (the wedge)

- **What:** 1 finished short clip cut from the creator's existing content, delivered free, no strings.
- **Price:** $0.
- **Purpose:** proof + foot in the door. This *is* the pitch. Every sample also grows our portfolio.
- **Ask after delivery:** "Want a few more like this each week?" → Rung 1.

## Rung 1 — Clipping Retainer (core offer)

- **What:** a set number of finished shorts per month from their long-form (podcast/YouTube), captioned and ready to post.
- **Price:** **$800–$2,000/mo** depending on volume (e.g. 8 / 12 / 20 clips) and turnaround.
- **Purpose:** predictable recurring revenue; the relationship that earns every upsell.

## Rung 2 — Full Short-Form Management

- **What:** Rung 1 + we handle posting, captions/hooks, thumbnails, hashtags, and a basic content calendar. They stop thinking about short-form entirely.
- **Price:** **+$500–$1,500/mo** on top of the clipping retainer.
- **Upsell trigger:** client likes the clips but is too busy to post them consistently.

## Rung 3 — Website / Funnel

- **What:** a landing page, sales page, or simple funnel for their course/community.
- **Price:** **one-time $1,500–$5,000+**, or bundled into the retainer.
- **Upsell trigger:** clips drive traffic but they have no good place to send it, or their site is weak.

## Rung 4 — AI Automation

- **What:** automations we build with these same tools — lead capture, email follow-up sequences, DM/comment triage, content repurposing pipelines, booking flows.
- **Price:** **setup fee + monthly** (scope-dependent).
- **Upsell trigger:** they're drowning in DMs/leads/admin — the same problem we're solving for ourselves here.

## Rung 5 — Growth Partner (full stack)

- **What:** everything above under one roof. We're their outsourced growth team.
- **Price:** **$3,000–$8,000+/mo** retainer.
- **Upsell trigger:** they trust us across multiple services and want one accountable partner.

## Pricing principles

- **Anchor on ROI, not hours.** One extra course sale can cover the whole retainer.
- **Land, then raise.** Early clients get "founder" pricing in exchange for a testimonial. New clients pay more as proof accumulates.
- **Bundle to climb.** Discount the next rung slightly when added to an existing retainer — makes the upsell feel like a deal.
