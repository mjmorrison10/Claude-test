# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

_Who we target first. The tighter this is, the better our prospecting and outreach work._

## The bullseye

A **male self-improvement coach or course seller** who:

- Sells something with margin: a **course, community, or coaching program** (money to pay us).
- Already produces **long-form content** — a podcast and/or YouTube channel — so raw footage to clip already exists.
- Is **active but inconsistent on short-form** (IG Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X). Posting rarely, or posting low-effort clips. This is the pain we relieve.
- Sits in the **follower sweet spot: ~10k–500k**. Big enough to afford us, small enough to still need help and not already have a full team.

## Niche focus

**Male self-improvement / "become a better man":** discipline, fitness, mindset, money/business for young men, dating & confidence, faith-based masculinity, Tate/TRW-adjacent creators.

We know this world. That's our edge — we can clip for *what converts to sales*, not just what looks good.

## Buying triggers (reasons they'd say yes now)

- Just launched or is about to launch a course/community and needs attention.
- Posts long podcasts but almost nothing short-form.
- Their shorts underperform their long-form (untapped clip potential).
- Growing fast and clearly overwhelmed / doing everything themselves.

## Disqualifiers (skip these)

- No product to sell (nothing to fund a retainer).
- Already has an obvious editing team producing high-volume, high-quality shorts.
- Pure controversy/rage-bait with no actual offer — hard to upsell, reputational risk.
- Under ~5k followers with no product — usually can't pay yet. (Keep a "nurture later" note.)

## Where they hang out (for prospecting)

- YouTube (search their niche + "podcast"), Spotify podcast charts (self-improvement).
- Instagram / TikTok / X creators in the space.
- Skool communities and course platforms in the men's development niche.

## The upsell read

For each lead, note the *obvious next need* beyond clips — no website? weak funnel? no email list? no lead-gen automation? That's the upsell we plant early and sell later.
