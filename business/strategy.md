# Strategy

_Last updated: 2026-07-07_

## The one-line version

Get in the door with **free/cheap short-form clips** for male self-improvement creators, then climb the **upsell ladder** to content management, websites, and AI automation — becoming their outsourced growth team.

## Why this works for us specifically

- **We already have the skill.** Turning long-form content into clips is proven, repeatable work we've done and gotten paid for.
- **We know the audience.** We've lived in the Tate / TRW / self-improvement world and done affiliate marketing in it. We speak the language, we know what content converts.
- **Clips are the perfect wedge.** Low commitment for the client, fast to deliver, easy to prove value ("here's a clip I already made you — free").
- **The niche has money.** Coaches and course sellers make high margins on info products; a $1–2k/mo retainer is nothing to them if it drives sales.

## The wedge → ladder model

```
FREE SAMPLE  →  CLIPPING RETAINER  →  FULL SHORT-FORM MGMT  →  WEBSITE/FUNNEL  →  AI AUTOMATION  →  GROWTH PARTNER
   (proof)         ($800–2k/mo)          (+posting/thumbs)       (one-time $$$)     (retainer $$$)     (everything)
```

Each rung earns trust that makes the next rung an easy yes. We never lead with the expensive stuff — we earn our way up.

See [`offers-and-pricing.md`](offers-and-pricing.md) for the detail on each rung.

## The two problems we're solving first

1. **No proof.** Old social accounts were banned; past work wasn't documented. **Fix:** every clip we make from now on gets logged in [`portfolio/`](../portfolio/). Reactivate *Come On Man Podcast* for a testimonial to seed credibility fast.
2. **No lead flow.** **Fix:** the [Free-Sample Outreach engine](../playbooks/free-sample-outreach.md) — a repeatable system that both finds leads and builds portfolio with every send.

## 90-day objective

- Land or re-sign **2–3 retainer clients**.
- Build a **portfolio of 15–20 logged clips** with at least one named testimonial.
- Prove the upsell path by selling **one** non-clipping service (website or automation) to an existing client.

## Guardrails

- **Nothing goes out without owner review** during setup. Drafts first, send on approval.
- **Never fabricate proof.** We rebuild the track record honestly — real clips, real results.
- **Warm before cold.** Always exhaust warm contacts and referrals before cold outreach; they convert far higher.
