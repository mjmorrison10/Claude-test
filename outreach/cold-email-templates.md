# Outreach Templates

_Starting points. Always personalize — reference the specific episode/moment. Keep it short, human, low-pressure. No corporate voice; talk like a person who watches their stuff._

---

## Warm reactivation — former client (Come On Man Podcast)

> Subject: been a minute
>
> Yo [Name] — hope things are going well with the show, been catching some of the recent episodes.
>
> Two quick things:
> 1. I put together a fresh clip from [episode] just because the [moment] bit was too good to leave buried — sending it over, no ask, it's yours.
> 2. If you've been happy with the clips I did for you, would you be open to a quick line I can use as a testimonial? Rebuilding my portfolio after some account stuff and your word would mean a lot.
>
> And if you ever want to turn the clip tap back on, I'm around. Either way, glad we're still in touch.
>
> — [You]

---

## Cold — free sample lead

> Subject: made you a clip (free)
>
> Hey [Name] — I clipped a short from your [episode/video] on [topic]. The part where you said "[hook line]" — cut it into a 40s vertical, captioned, ready to post. It's attached, yours to use, no catch.
>
> I make these for men's self-improvement creators — turning your long episodes into a steady stream of shorts so you're not leaving all that reach on the table.
>
> Want me to send a few more from your recent stuff this week?
>
> — [You]

---

## Follow-up #1 (no reply, ~3–4 days)

> Subject: re: made you a clip
>
> Bumping this in case it got buried — the clip's still yours either way. Happy to send 3 more from [recent episode] if you want to see the volume. No pressure.

---

## Follow-up #2 (no reply, ~1 week later — last touch)

> Subject: last one, promise
>
> I'll get out of your inbox after this. If short-form ever becomes a priority, I'd love to be the one handling it — I already know your content. Door's open whenever.

---

## After a "yes, send more" → soft pitch retainer

> Love it. Easiest way to do this: I take your episodes as they drop and turn each one into [X] shorts a month, captioned and ready. Runs about [$___]/mo. Want me to send the details and we start with this week's episode?

---

## Voice notes
- Lowercase subjects and casual tone fit this niche better than polished corporate copy.
- Never open with "I'm a video editor." Open with the value already delivered.
- One clear ask per message. Make saying yes take zero effort.
